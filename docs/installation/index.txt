<h2 id="updating">Updating<a class="anchor" aria-hidden tabindex="-1" href="#updating">#</a></h2>
<p>Inkiva checks for updates on launch. When an update is published, the app downloads it in the background and installs it on the next restart. Portable installs must be updated by downloading the new zip.</p>
