Markdown editor comparison

Inkiva vs Typora vs MarkText

A practical comparison of three desktop Markdown editors for people who want to write in a rendered document while keeping their files portable.

Choose Inkiva if…

You use Windows or macOS, prefer editing directly in the rendered document, want a folder-and-document workflow, and value local history, autosave and crash recovery around ordinary Markdown files.

Choose Typora if…

You want Typora’s established commercial product and are comfortable with its one-time license. Typora also publishes Linux builds, which Inkiva does not currently provide.

Choose MarkText if…

You prefer the MIT license or need Linux packages. MarkText is the upstream project on which Inkiva is based; Inkiva is a separately branded and actively developed derivative with its own product direction and GPL-3.0 project license.

Important context

Inkiva does not claim to be universally better than Typora or MarkText. Its current fit is specifically Windows/macOS users who want document-first WYSIWYG editing, standard Markdown portability and additional local document navigation and recovery. Linux, mobile, cloud sync and team collaboration are not current Inkiva release targets.
