<h2 id="updating">Updating<a class="anchor" aria-hidden tabindex="-1" href="#updating">#</a></h2>
<p>Inkiva checks for updates on launch (this can be disabled under <strong>Preferences → General → Updates</strong>). When an update is published, the app downloads it in the background and installs on next restart.</p>
<p>Portable installs and the AppImage do not auto-update — re-download the latest artifact when you want to upgrade.</p>
