More than a Markdown editor

Everything around the file stays lightweight.

Inkiva adds useful navigation and recovery around ordinary Markdown files — without turning a folder into a proprietary workspace.

01

Write beautifully

Headings, emphasis, links, lists and code render in place, keeping you in the flow. What you see is what you are writing.

As you type
Bold, italics, links and code all render instantly.
02

Navigate naturally

Open files and folders, and keep your recent documents close. Everything you need is just a few clicks away.

Keep documents connected

Use standard relative Markdown links and headings. Backlinks and repair prompts help you keep your documents in shape.

Keep your work safe

Your files live on your device. Local history, autosave and crash recovery help protect your work while you write.

Markdown, kept portable

Standard Markdown, still yours.

Inkiva does not invent a private syntax or lock your writing inside the app. Leave Inkiva whenever you like: links stay links, and your document stays a document.
